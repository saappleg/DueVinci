Privacy & Compliance

Privacy Policy & Data Retention

Effective Date: February 20, 2026 • Last Updated: October 17, 2026

1. Introduction & Scope

At DueVinci ("we", "our", or "the Service"), we take student privacy and data security seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the DueVinci web application at duevinci.tech.

By using DueVinci, you consent to the practices described in this policy. We do not sell your personal data to third parties or use student coursework for behavioral advertising.

2. Information We Collect

A. Account & Authentication Data

When you register or sign in, we collect your email address and authentication credentials managed securely through Supabase Auth (including WebAuthn/Passkey public key credentials where enabled). You may also provide a display name and an optional profile photo.

B. Student Academic Content

We store the academic records you explicitly enter, including course names, term folders, assignment titles, due dates, completion and partial-progress values, assignment instructions and subtasks, grade percentages, course credit hours, weighted grade categories, custom calendar events, and study notes/scratchpads. Repeated coursework is saved as individual coursework entries. Flashcard review progress (such as repetitions and next review dates) is stored on this device and synced to your account when you are online so it can follow you across devices. If you use the optional Nursing Hub, its workspace can include rotation dates and skill status, fictional care-plan drafts, dosage-practice progress, custom flashcards, and anatomy review progress. Do not enter real patient information into the Nursing Hub, including information that could identify a patient.

C. AI Processing Inputs

When you use syllabus parsing or the Socratic Study Companion, we send the minimum input needed to provide that feature through a secure server-side Supabase Edge Function to Google Gemini. This can include syllabus text or images, your Tutor question and recent page-only conversation context, and—when you select a course—its description, learning objectives, saved notes, and up to 20 assignment titles and due dates. Tutor answers may return excerpts checked against those selected course materials. DueVinci does not store Tutor conversations, but stores account-level request counts to enforce usage limits. DueVinci does not use this content to train its own models. Google’s handling of API prompts and responses depends on the applicable Gemini service tier and terms; do not submit highly sensitive personal information to AI features.

D. Preferences and Nursing Hub Workspace

UI settings such as your active theme, preferred date format, calendar week start, timer audio profiles, workspace visibility, dashboard layout, and Pomodoro durations are cached in browser storage. Nursing Hub workspace data is also stored in browser storage and, for signed-in users, synchronizes while online through the protected Supabase user_preferences record associated with your account. The database applies account-level row access controls.

If you enable the optional Pro study-plan calibration feature, DueVinci records task and course IDs, task types, estimated task minutes, focus minutes, and timestamps in local browser storage and syncs this history through that account preference record. The newest 180 focus sessions and 120 task completions are retained; older entries may be dropped when a limit is reached. Turning calibration off pauses new recording but does not delete saved history; you can clear it in Settings. Estimates use recent history from the last 90 days, but that calculation window is not an automatic deletion period.

E. Connected Services & Subscription Data

If you connect Canvas, we process your Canvas instance address and access token to retrieve coursework for the courses you select. Canvas assignments are imported directly; they do not go through the separate WGU/Maestro browser importer's row-review step. The token is encrypted server-side and removed when you disconnect or delete your account. After Canvas sync, DueVinci maintains a server-only snapshot of assignment ID, course ID, assignment title, grade, score, points possible, posted time, and observation time so it can compare grades over time. These snapshots are kept whether or not grade alerts are enabled; they do not contain Canvas submission text or comments, and remain until account deletion even if you disconnect Canvas. Posted-grade alerts are a separate opt-in.

If you connect Google Calendar or Outlook, we store encrypted provider tokens and import event titles and start dates from the provider's default calendar for a rolling window (30 days back through 180 days ahead), up to 5,000 events per sync. DueVinci runs an initial sync after you connect; you start later syncs. These read-only syncs do not write to your provider. Disconnecting deletes DueVinci's saved tokens and hides the imported Google or Outlook events, but does not delete their event rows; the rows are retained until event or account deletion.

If you connect the SNHU Brightspace calendar feed, its feed URL is encrypted server-side and imported event titles and dates are saved in your calendar; disconnecting removes the saved feed URL but does not automatically remove imported events. If you subscribe, Stripe processes payment details. DueVinci stores subscription status and Stripe customer or subscription identifiers, but not full payment-card numbers. If you enable browser reminders, a browser push subscription endpoint and its keys may be stored so notices can be delivered.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To render your personal dashboard, courses, grade simulator, and calendar timeline.
  • To compute cumulative GPA, course completion percentages, and 7-day workload distributions.
  • To authenticate your session securely across multiple devices.
  • To deliver syllabus parsing and the Socratic Study Companion through Google Gemini APIs.
  • To calculate the Daily Brief from your own coursework and calendar records, and to meter Tutor requests against your plan allowance.
  • To save and display optional Nursing Hub rotation tracking and educational practice progress that you choose to enter.
  • To connect and import the Canvas coursework you expressly authorize.
  • To sync event titles and dates from Google Calendar and Outlook after you connect and when you start later syncs, and from an SNHU Brightspace feed when you connect and request a feed sync.
  • When enabled, to refresh selected Canvas courses and compare server-only grade snapshots so posted-grade changes can be sent to subscribed browser devices.
  • To administer trials, subscriptions, billing support, and browser reminders when you enable them.
  • To provide customer support when you reach out via our support channels.

5. Account Deletion & Right to Be Forgotten

You have the right to permanently purge your account and all associated data at any time. DueVinci provides an automated, self-service Delete Account & Purge Data option inside the Settings modal.

What account deletion does:

When you confirm account deletion, DueVinci cancels outstanding subscriptions, removes the account, primary academic records, support tickets, error reports, flashcard progress, Nursing Hub workspace data, optional study-plan history, Tutor usage counts, Canvas and calendar connection records, imported calendar events, Canvas grade snapshots, and profile photo, and clears local app storage on the browser where deletion is requested. An offline copy on another device cannot be cleared remotely; clear DueVinci site data on that device as well. Limited records held by payment, email, AI, or infrastructure providers may remain subject to their own policies and applicable legal obligations.

6. Security & Encryption Practices

We employ industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS): All network traffic between your client browser and our servers is encrypted with modern TLS ciphers.
  • Row Level Security (RLS): Database tables enforce PostgreSQL RLS policies ensuring users can only read and mutate records matching their authenticated UUID.
  • Protected API Gateways: Gemini API keys are isolated within server-side Supabase Vault secrets and are never exposed in client bundles.
  • Private Credentials: Canvas access tokens, Google and Outlook OAuth tokens, and SNHU Brightspace feed URLs are encrypted before server-side storage. Canvas grade snapshots are kept in a server-only table and contain grade fields rather than submission text or comments. Profile photos are protected by per-user access controls.

7. Third-Party Service Providers

DueVinci utilizes trusted cloud infrastructure partners solely to operate the service:

  • Supabase Inc.: Database hosting, user authentication, and serverless Edge Functions.
  • Google Cloud (Gemini API): Natural language and optical document processing for syllabus parsing.
  • Stripe: Payment processing and subscription management; payment-card information is handled by Stripe.
  • Canvas LMS instances selected by you: Coursework retrieval using the access token you provide.
  • Google Calendar API and Microsoft Graph: Read-only import of event titles and start dates from your default calendar. An initial sync follows account connection; you start later syncs.
  • SNHU Brightspace calendar feed: Import of events from the private feed URL you provide.
  • Resend: Delivery of support emails when you submit a support request.

8. Your Choices, Rights & Contact

You can update much of your account information in Settings, disconnect Canvas, Google Calendar, Outlook, or Brightspace, export your data, or delete your account. Disconnecting Google or Outlook removes DueVinci's saved tokens and hides imported events; Google token revocation is attempted, while disconnecting Outlook removes DueVinci's tokens but does not revoke Microsoft-side app consent. You can manage that consent through the relevant Microsoft account or organization controls. Disconnecting Brightspace removes the saved feed URL, but imported events remain until event or account deletion. You can also turn off study-plan calibration or clear its saved history in Settings. To request access, correction, deletion, or information about how your data is handled, contact support@duevinci.tech. We may need to verify your request before acting on it.
