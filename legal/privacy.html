Privacy & Compliance

Privacy Policy & Data Retention

Effective Date: February 20, 2026 • Last Updated: August 23, 2026

1. Introduction & Scope

At DueVinci ("we", "our", or "the Service"), we take student privacy and data security seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the DueVinci web application at duevinci.tech.

By using DueVinci, you consent to the practices described in this policy. We do not sell your personal data to third parties or use student coursework for behavioral advertising.

2. Information We Collect

A. Account & Authentication Data

When you register or sign in, we collect your email address and authentication credentials managed securely through Supabase Auth (including WebAuthn/Passkey public key credentials where enabled).

B. Student Academic Content

We store the academic records you explicitly enter, including: course names, term folders, assignment titles, due dates, completion statuses, grade percentages, custom calendar events, and study notes/scratchpads.

C. AI Document Processing Inputs

When you upload syllabus PDFs or lesson screenshots for AI scheduling, the extracted text or image payload is transmitted ephemerally to our secure Supabase Edge Function to interface with Google Gemini. Files and prompt inputs are processed in-flight and are not used to train generalized foundation models.

D. Client-Side Preferences (Local Storage)

UI configuration such as your active theme (dark/light/Greek mode), preferred date format, timer audio profiles, and Pomodoro durations are cached on your browser. For signed-in users, non-sensitive preferences may also synchronize through a protected account preference record.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To render your personal dashboard, courses, grade simulator, and calendar timeline.
  • To compute cumulative GPA, course completion percentages, and 7-day workload distributions.
  • To authenticate your session securely across multiple devices.
  • To deliver automated syllabus parsing and quiz generation via Google Gemini APIs.
  • To provide customer support when you reach out via our support channels.

4. Data Retention & Storage Policy

We maintain strict data retention standards to ensure you remain in complete control of your academic data:

Data Category Retention Period Storage Location
Account & Profile Retained until account deletion requested Supabase (PostgreSQL with RLS)
Coursework & Grades Retained until deleted by user or account removed Supabase DB + IndexedDB Offline Cache
Uploaded Syllabus Files Parsed ephemerally in memory; raw files are not permanently stored Edge Function Memory (Ephemeral)
Local Preferences Persistent until changed, account deletion, or browser-data clearing Protected preference record + browser cache
Support Tickets Resolved or closed tickets are deleted 90 days after resolution Supabase (PostgreSQL with RLS)
Browser Error Reports Deleted automatically after 90 days Supabase (restricted diagnostic table)

5. Account Deletion & Right to Be Forgotten

You have the right to permanently purge your account and all associated data at any time. DueVinci provides an automated, self-service Delete Account & Purge Data option inside the Settings modal.

Immediate Deletion Guarantee:

When you confirm account deletion, your user record, enrolled courses, assignments, custom calendar events, and offline caches are permanently erased immediately across database tables and local storage.

6. Security & Encryption Practices

We employ industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS): All network traffic between your client browser and our servers is encrypted with modern TLS ciphers.
  • Row Level Security (RLS): Database tables enforce PostgreSQL RLS policies ensuring users can only read and mutate records matching their authenticated UUID.
  • Protected API Gateways: Gemini API keys are isolated within server-side Supabase Vault secrets and are never exposed in client bundles.

7. Third-Party Service Providers

DueVinci utilizes trusted cloud infrastructure partners solely to operate the service:

  • Supabase Inc.: Database hosting, user authentication, and serverless Edge Functions.
  • Google Cloud (Gemini API): Natural language and optical document processing for syllabus parsing.

8. Contact & Questions

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to our team at support@duevinci.tech.
